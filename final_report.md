## 9. Business Case Justification
### Pricing Justification
The proposed fee of AED 450.00 per transaction recovers 128.6% of the calculated unit cost of AED 350.00, providing a cost recovery surplus. The total customer cost, including third-party and mandatory charges, is AED 480.00. This represents a fee reduction of AED 50.00, or 10.0%, from the current fee of AED 500.00, reducing the annual revenue forecast by AED 600,000.00. The proposed fee is 4.7% above the average GCC/international benchmark of AED 430.00 and requires benchmark validation before approval. With 60% automation and a 20-minute average processing time, there is further efficiency headroom that could support a lower fee at the next renewal period without reducing cost recovery.

### Government Financial Impact
Annual revenue decreases from AED 6,000,000.00 to AED 5,400,000.00, a reduction of AED 600,000.00. Cost recovery is maintained above 100% at the proposed fee level.

### Customer Impact
Customers pay AED 50.00 less per transaction than under the current fee. The total mandatory cost remains competitive with GCC/regional benchmarks once third-party charges are included. The fee reduction supports affordability for SMEs.
